# Incident at Byte Bistro

**Category:** Digital Archaeology  
**Difficulty:** Medium

The company café's POS terminal allegedly caused a production outage. The terminal claims it was at lunch. The timestamps are having a disagreement.

## Your objective
Investigate the supplied artifacts and recover the incident's submission token. Every clue you need is in this folder; management's statements are not evidence.

## Files
- `chat.txt` — the primary evidence file.
- `chat.txt` and `access.log` — cross-check timestamps and workstation behavior.

## Rules and tools
Submit a flag in `nexus{...}` format. You may use a modern browser and Python 3; standard-library solutions are preferred. Do not execute unknown binaries—none are required. For image/audio puzzles, a small Python script is sufficient. No external account or paid tool is needed.

## Hint
Trust reproducible evidence over confident statements from fictional management. If two artifacts disagree, determine which one can be independently verified.
