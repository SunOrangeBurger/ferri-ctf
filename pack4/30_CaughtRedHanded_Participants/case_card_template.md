# CaughtRedHanded — participant case card

Ask the organizer for your sealed case card and matching artifact variant. It contains a unique case ID, one-time nonce, and a small observation task tied to your exact variant.

Submit the case ID, decoded response, checksum, and a concise evidence transcript identifying exact pixel/record positions. A bare flag without reproducible evidence is not accepted. The organizer may ask one brief oral follow-up.

This is a process-integrity check, not a claim that AI use can be detected perfectly. No static challenge can reliably prove AI use by itself.
